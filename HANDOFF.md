# Foothold — GPU Computing for AI · HANDOFF

The single source of truth for the course's status. Read this first in any new session.

## What this is
A 1-credit, skill-based (0-0-2) Foothold course: **why AI runs on GPUs, and how the CPU hands work to the GPU**.
- It starts with the maths of AI (no GPU yet), then why AI needs a GPU, GPU internals, CPU–GPU teamwork, speed tricks and real projects.
- It was built 2026-10-17/18. The syllabus is in `Class - aishu/` (the v2 draft of 2026-11-01: 5 units of 6 hours each).
- It comes with a course book, **A Billion Small Steps: GPU Computing for AI and Beyond, from Zero** (called “Step Up” until 7 Nov 2026) (see "The book" below).

  - `notebooks/CoreLab01_…` to `CoreLab12_…`: one notebook per two-hour session, parts restart at A (mapping in the docstring of `build_labs.py`).
  - `notebooks/SideTrails_Step1_…` to `SideTrails_Step5_…` and `SideTrails_GPUs_Beyond_AI.ipynb`: optional.
  - `lab1.py` … `lab5c.py` are now only the **sources** of the cells (`nbparts.py` imports them without writing the old notebooks). Edit the text there; `build_labs.py` slices, relabels and adds the new cells (version cell, backprop check, overfitting, CNN, attention, INT8 with a torchao fallback). Steps 2–5 code still comes from the book via `bookcells.py`; the new Step 1 cells come from `book/src/*.html` via `nbparts.book_html`.

- **Testing a notebook:** `python tools/notebooks/run_nb.py notebooks/<file>.ipynb`.
- Palette rule: never name a variable `C` inside a widget (it hides the colour palette).
- A `<` inside a section title must be written `&lt;` (titles are inserted into JSX).
